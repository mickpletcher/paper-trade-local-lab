# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

TradeForge contributors must keep discussion professional, technical, and welcoming. Constructive disagreement is encouraged; personal attacks are not.

Expected behavior includes:

* giving and accepting specific, evidence-based feedback
* respecting different experience levels and viewpoints
* protecting private information and credentials
* keeping issues, reviews, and discussions focused on the project
* acknowledging mistakes and correcting them promptly

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, lock conversations, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and whether the behavior is repeated.

Use GitHub's private reporting and abuse tools for sensitive conduct reports. Do not expose private evidence in a public issue.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the TradeForge project.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
